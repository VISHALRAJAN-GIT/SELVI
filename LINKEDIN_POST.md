## Introducing Selvi: Making Government Services Easier to Navigate

I’m excited to share **Selvi**, a Tamil-first, multilingual government service guide designed to help people navigate important online public services with greater confidence.

Many government portals are useful but difficult to understand, especially for first-time digital-service users. Selvi helps by turning complicated journeys into simple, readable and voice-supported steps.

### What Selvi offers

- Tamil-first experience with support for Hindi, Bengali, Marathi and Telugu
- State/UT selection and service discovery
- Beginner-friendly preparation checklists and step-by-step guides
- Exact Tamil narration for the complete Aadhaar guide
- Tamil audio support across the service directory
- Official government links and source references
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- High-contrast yellow-and-black interface
- Keyboard-friendly controls and visible text alternatives
- Safety reminders to protect Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, passwords and bank details
- No form filling, credential collection or application submission on behalf of users

The initial service directory includes Aadhaar, voter services, passport applications, DigiLocker, Instant e-PAN and Tamil Nadu services such as community certificates, income certificates and family-card/PDS services.

Selvi is designed around a simple principle:

> Explain clearly. Point to the official source. Let the user stay in control.

### Engineering highlights

- React, TypeScript and Vite
- Express/tRPC server architecture
- Static, source-backed service catalog
- Automated tests for catalog integrity, language data, audio routing, safety boundaries and extension behavior
- Optional Chrome helper for deterministic page guidance on the official PM Vishwakarma site
- Production build and Chrome extension build verified successfully

The latest verification completed with **34 passing tests**, successful TypeScript validation, successful production and extension builds, and a healthy `/api/health` endpoint.
