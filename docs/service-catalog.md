# Selvi service-catalog sources and coverage

Checked on **2026-10-01** for the starter release. This is a source list for Selvi's plain-language guides, not an official government directory or exhaustive national catalog. Selvi's steps summarize public official information; each official portal remains authoritative and its current screen should be followed.

## Language-choice evidence

Selvi puts Tamil first at the user's request, then shows the other four in descending Census share order. These figures are historical mother-tongue shares, not a current survey of language use, fluency, or service demand.

## More official discovery

- [India.gov.in State/UT Services](https://www.india.gov.in/services/state) is the Government of India's directory for state and UT service discovery.
- [Integrated Government Online Directory — State/UT Government](https://igod.gov.in/sg/states) provides official government website links.

The website's eight entries are practical first-release choices from broad identity, election, travel, document, tax, civil-certificate and food-security tasks. Official usage or popularity rankings were not verified for seven entries. DigiLocker's official homepage showed scale counters when checked; that is not a comparative ranking of Indian services. The UI therefore labels the catalog a starter set and never claims it contains all or the most-used government services.

## Translation and safety boundary

Tamil is the source language for the service guide copy. Static Hindi, Bengali, Marathi and Telugu copies were generated with a structured translation pass and preserve item counts; they still need review by native-language public-service editors before being represented as publication-ready. Users are explicitly told translations are not official and to follow the linked portal. Selvi does not collect credentials, OTPs or application details, does not inspect or operate government forms, and cannot guarantee eligibility or an outcome.
