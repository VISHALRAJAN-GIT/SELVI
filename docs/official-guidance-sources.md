# Official PM Vishwakarma guidance sources

Checked: 2026-10-01

## Primary sources

- Official portal: <https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/>
- Official FAQ: <https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/FAQ>
- Official registration e-booklet (PDF): <https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/CDN/MiscFiles/Registration%20e-Booklet.pdf>

## Facts used in Selvi

The official FAQ states that an individual seeking scheme benefits may register on the official portal. It lists Aadhaar, a mobile number, bank details, and a ration card (or family Aadhaar details when a ration card is unavailable) among the registration information/documents. It also says people may seek help at a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC), MSME-Development and Facilitation Office (MSME-DFO), or District Industries Centre (DIC).

The official homepage exposes public scheme information, eligibility/trade/benefit content, Applicant/Beneficiary Login, and CSC Login. The walkthrough must not direct a general applicant to CSC Login: it is intended for CSC users/operators. For new enrollment, the guide points users to official instructions and in-person assistance instead of guessing at a changing portal workflow.

The e-booklet URL was checked but could not be text-extracted in this environment. Do not treat it as a reviewed step-by-step source until its contents are independently inspected.

## Safety and implementation boundary

Selvi only guides. It does not collect Aadhaar or bank data, read values typed into government forms, enter credentials, click portal controls, or submit applications. The optional browser helper is user-clicked, runs only on the official PM Vishwakarma domain, reads visible navigation labels only, and shows a reversible pointer suggestion locally. The website guide provides source links and warns users to enter private details only on the official government service.

Government pages can change; users should verify instructions on the official portal or with a CSC/MSME-DFO/DIC before acting.
